Google and Meta ad management for local service businesses

Breyes Agency runs paid advertising for local service businesses: home services contractors, trades, and similar companies whose growth depends on a steady flow of booked jobs rather than brand awareness. The work covers Google Search Ads and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Ads, run as one connected system rather than a set of disconnected campaigns handed off between specialists.

About this service

Who this is for

Local service businesses that already have a working offer and want more of it: roofers, HVAC companies, plumbers, electricians, landscapers, remodelers, and similar trades where the average job is worth enough to justify paid media.

What's included

Six things, run as one job rather than split across vendors: paid ad management on Google and Meta, the creative (copy and visuals) behind each ad, the strategy deciding which platform and offer and audience come first, funnel and landing-page optimization so clicks turn into calls, tracking and analytics so every lead is counted, and audience targeting focused on the people in the business's actual service area. See the full service breakdown.

How a paid ad budget works

The advertising budget is separate from the management fee and goes directly to Google or Meta. For Meta, a workable starting point is around $1,500 a month, with most active accounts spending between $2,000 and $5,000. For Google Search, the floor sits closer to $2,000 a month, with most spending $3,000 to $8,000. Below those thresholds, a campaign rarely gathers enough conversion data to improve on its own, which is part of why campaigns are run on a minimum three-month term.

How an engagement starts

Every engagement opens with a conversation, not a pitch. The first call is about understanding where the business actually stands: what's working, what isn't, and what the real bottleneck is, as opposed to the obvious symptom. From there, a plan comes back covering which platform, which offer, which audience, and in what order, along with a realistic read on what 90 days can look like. See how the process works.

The system left behind

This isn't a service rented indefinitely with nothing to show for it. Over the course of an engagement, the business ends up owning a working system: live conversion tracking, a dashboard that can be read in under ten seconds, automatic follow-up so a lead never sits cold, and a clear, visible cost per booked job.

Websites that support the ads

Paid ads only perform as well as the page they send traffic to. Two options are available: a basic website (up to five pages, mobile-first, contact form, on-page SEO basics, one round of revisions) and a higher-converting website (everything in the basic package plus conversion-focused copywriting, analytics wired directly into the layout, speed and Core Web Vitals optimization, and booking tools built in). See website pricing and scope.

Contract terms and who does the work

Ad management runs on a fixed term of 3, 6, or 12 months, because a campaign needs real time to gather data, settle, and improve before results are fairly judged. One person does the work directly, end to end, rather than routing the account through a junior team member or an offshore fulfillment desk.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SEO, GEO, and AEO?

SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search engines. GEO focuses on helping AI systems understand and potentially surface your business. AEO focuses on structuring useful answers to customer questions.

Do I need all three services?

Not every business needs the same approach. We'll review your website and recommend priorities based on your goals, current visibility, and opportunities.

Can you guarantee that my business will appear first on Google or ChatGPT?

No. Search rankings and AI-generated recommendations depend on many factors outside our control. We focus on sound optimization practices and measurable improvements.

How long does SEO take to work?

Results vary depending on competition, website condition, industry, and the work performed. SEO is generally a longer-term investment rather than an instant-results service.

Do you build or improve websites too?

Yes. If a website needs structural, design, content, or conversion improvements, we can discuss those alongside the search optimization strategy. See our website packages.
